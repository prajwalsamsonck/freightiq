# Project context

FreightIQ was developed by our team for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.

The project progressed through our college’s institutional selection process. It was one of the top 50 teams selected by the college and forwarded for the final-stage evaluation, where the team submitted the project idea and presentation through the official government portal. The final result was pending at the time of this repository publication.

## Problem statement

Bulk-cargo procurement teams must make chartering decisions while freight rates, vessel availability, port congestion, weather risk, and commodity conditions change together. A spreadsheet or single market indicator does not provide enough context for comparing the cost and downside of waiting, chartering immediately, or choosing a different vessel class.

## Project response

FreightIQ combines route-aware market history, forecasts, operational context, constraint-based charter optimization, scenario simulation, and explainable evidence in a single decision workflow.

## Demonstration scope

The current public build uses reproducible synthetic data for routes into Paradip, Visakhapatnam, and Kolkata/Haldia. The architecture includes adapter boundaries for future live freight, AIS, commodity, port, and weather feeds, but no commercial credentials or private data are included in this repository.
